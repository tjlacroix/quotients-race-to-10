Quotients: Race to 10

Build fractions strategically. Get as close to 10 as possible without going over.

How to Play & Division Language
  1. Each player gets four digit cards each round.
  2. Use all four digits exactly once to make two fractions.
  3. Choose which fraction is the dividend and which is the divisor.
  4. Submit your division. The quotient is simplified automatically.
  5. Your quotient is added to your running total. You cannot go over 10.
  6. Exactly 10 wins immediately. Otherwise, after 5 rounds, the player closest to 10 wins.

No card switching. If no legal arrangement can keep your total at or below 10, use “No Legal Move” to score 0 for the round.
